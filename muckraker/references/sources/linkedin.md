# LinkedIn

LinkedIn has no public API, puts most pages behind a login, and usually answers direct fetches with HTTP 403. Work from search results, and from the company's own website and public job boards where LinkedIn gives nothing. Mark everything from LinkedIn search results low confidence.

## Checks

1. **Company page:** search `"<company name>"` restricted to linkedin.com and record what the result shows: stated industry, headcount band, headquarters, founded year, website, description. Check that the website matches the entity record.
2. **Consistency:** note where the page disagrees with the entity record (a different headquarters or founding year, a headcount band out of line with the company's size).
3. **Leadership:** open the company's own leadership or about page and record the executives and titles it lists; this is the most current source. Then search `"<company name>"` together with CEO, founder, CFO, and director, restricted to linkedin.com, and note any name or title that differs from the company page. Record names and titles only, for the coordinator to compare with registry filings.
4. **Hiring signals:** search `"<company name>" jobs` restricted to linkedin.com; if that returns nothing, use the company's careers page or public job boards. Note whether the company appears to be hiring and in which locations, as a sign of activity.
