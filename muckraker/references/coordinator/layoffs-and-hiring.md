# Layoffs and hiring, run by you

While the agents work, run `python <skill folder>/scripts/mr.py warn "<legal or trading name>" > "<run folder>/warn.jsonl"` and `python <skill folder>/scripts/mr.py jobs <careers page URL> --history --offices "<office city>" "<metro area>" ... > "<run folder>/jobs.jsonl"`. `warn` returns US WARN layoff notices (filed for layoffs of 50 or more at one site) from the states that publish them as open data, California and Texas so far, and its `warn_summary` names the states searched: list the others where the company has a US office under Gaps unless the Press agent covered them.

`jobs` finds the company's Greenhouse, Lever, or Ashby board, counts its open roles by department and location, and with `--history` counts them in up to 12 monthly archived copies of the board page (approximate; Ashby boards have no archived list). For Ashby and Lever boards, `jobs_posting_ages` tallies the open roles by the month they were posted: a proxy for recent hiring where no archived trend exists.

A `jobs_board_guessed` record means the board was found from the domain name: check that its locations are the company's offices before using it. When the domain's name has boards on more than one provider (another company can own the same name), `jobs_board_candidates` lists them with their main locations, and the script picks the one whose locations match the offices in the facts file (`--offices`; give the metro area as well as the suburb, since boards write "Los Angeles, CA" for an office in Hawthorne), and puts `office_matches` on a single guessed board, with a warning when none of its roles is in an office given, or reports `jobs_board_ambiguous`: rerun with `provider:token` for the company's own board.

When the careers page refuses the script (`jobs_page_unreadable`), `jobs` still tries boards named after the domain; take the board from those guesses alone, leaving the careers page unfetched. `jobs_board_unsupported` names an applicant tracking system the command cannot read (Comeet, common at Israeli companies, Workable, Workday, and others): count the open roles on the careers page by hand if it opens in a browser, and say under Gaps that no trend was measured. In SKILL.md step 4, compare both with the headcount and growth claims.
