# Form D, run by you

When the user has given the SEC contact, run `python <skill folder>/scripts/mr.py --user-agent "<SEC User-Agent>" formd "<legal name>" "<former or US entity name>" "<founder's surname>" ... > "<run folder>/formd.jsonl"` before sending the agents (a few seconds per name). Search people by surname, or by the full legal name a Form D gives (filings use full legal names, so a short form or nickname finds nothing); the people found in a company's first Form D are the names to add for a second run. Leave out a common surname on its own, which returns dozens of unrelated filings; search the full name instead. A company whose parent is incorporated outside the US (an Israeli Ltd, for example) usually raises through that parent and files no Form D, so finding none is normal there and says nothing about the US subsidiary; only a US parent (a "flip") files them for its rounds.

It runs EDGAR full-text search for Form D filings naming each one and reads every filing found: the issuer, its state and year of incorporation, address, former names, industry, the amount sold and offered with the filer's note on them (`sales_clarification`, which often explains a gap with the announced round, such as SAFEs converted into it), the date of first sale, the number of investors, and the related persons with their roles (`formd_filing`, names and roles only, never their addresses), and a `formd_summary` grouping the filings by issuer.

Add each issuer that is the company or a group company (match it by name, address, and related persons) to the entity record with its state of formation, and leave out `investor_vehicle` issuers (a pooled fund, or an issuer named "a Series of", SPV, or Fund, such as "<company> a Series of <platform> LLC"), which invest in the company; check any other issuer whose name is not the company's, such as a syndicate LLC, the same way.

Give the file's path to the SEC EDGAR, US state registry, and Startup agents. If the user declined the SEC contact, or Python is not available, skip this: the agents search Form D with the web fetch tool as their source files say.
