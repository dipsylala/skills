# GLEIF ownership, run by you

You already ran `mr.py gleif <LEI>` in SKILL.md step 1. Run it also for each parent, acquisition-company, and acquired-subsidiary name in the entity record that has an LEI of its own (`mr.py gleif "<name>"` finds it), since a group's holding companies often have one when the operating company does not. Write `<run folder>/gleif.md` from the records, in the brief's report format, working through the checks in [gleif.md](../sources/gleif.md): the entity record, the parent chain or the `exception_reason` where it stops, the subsidiaries and their countries, and any `gleif_flag` record (an offshore parent) as a red flag quoting the parent's name and country. A `gleif_lapsed` record means the LEI is not maintained: say so under Facts, and check its legal name against the register before using it. A company with no LEI is a result: say so and list the searches you ran. If the script fails, send the GLEIF ownership agent instead.
